Modifications: discard records ending before 1900, preserve source coordinate precision, retain selected properties, convert explicit GW year/month/day fields to ISO dates, and normalize winding for D3 spherical rendering. No invented historical geometries. Full download SHA-256 and feature counts are recorded in provenance.json.

The dataset represents the authors' coding choices for independent states and dependencies. It is not a complete catalog of sovereign claims, wartime occupation lines, or every microstate. Disputed territories follow source coding, including the 2014 Crimea change. Rendering a territory is not an endorsement of a sovereignty claim. Dates of historical events, international recognition and dataset coding may differ.
